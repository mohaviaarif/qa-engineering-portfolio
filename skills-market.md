# skills-market.md
Source: P@SHA Skills Survey 2025 (pages 18, 21, 22) + 10 live Pakistan QA job postings (Day 1) + 15 more live Pakistan QA job postings (Day 5)

**Data notes**
- Day 1 = 10 posts. Day 5 = 15 posts. Combined = 25 posts (assumes no post appears in both scans).
- In the Day 5 matrix, ❌ means "not mentioned in the post," not "not required."
- 4 of the 15 Day 5 posts did not name the company. Sample sizes are small, so treat percentages as direction, not precision.
- Day 5 posts: 6 internships, 1 graduate trainee, the rest junior/associate/engineer roles.

**Day 5 automation detail:** 7 of 15 posts name an automation tool; 3 more mention automation without naming one ("basics a plus," "teachable," or generic frameworks), so 10 of 15 mention automation in some way. 5 of 15 name no tool at all.

Only 3 of 15 Day 5 posts name a language (JavaScript, Java, "Java or Python"). One more post tests JavaScript and Flutter apps but does not list a language as a requirement, so it is not counted.

**Key finding (Day 1):** Playwright (40%) beat Python (10%) 4-to-1 in the first 10 postings — supported TypeScript/Playwright over a Python-based stack.

**Key finding (Day 5):** Automation demand is more mixed in the larger sample. Selenium and Appium are each named in 4 of 15 posts, Playwright in 2. Combined (25 posts), Selenium and Appium lead at 28% each, with Playwright close behind at 24%. Playwright remains a top-3 tool, but it is not a clear winner. The locked stack is unchanged.

## Database
- Relational (PostgreSQL, MySQL, MS SQL) — dominant, highest demand across all experience levels
- Non-relational (MongoDB) — growing, but secondary for QA entry roles
- SQL explicitly named in only 2 of 15 Day 5 posts (13%); 5 of 25 combined (20%).

## Testing Methodologies (job posts)
**Day 1 (10 posts):** Regression (70%), Manual/Verification (60%), Functional (40%), Integration (40%), API (40%), Exploratory (30%), End-to-End (30%)

**Day 5 (15 posts):** Manual testing 14/15 (93%), API/Postman 8/15 (53%). Regression, exploratory, and integration were not tracked in the Day 5 matrix.

**Combined manual testing:** 20 of 25 posts (80%).
